Cross-Model Observations

46 · Jiho NamProduct Security

Looking across all of these, one pattern stands out to me. Almost every major issue this month involved a vendor firmware or driver change interacting with something on our side: the storage firmware and the timeout, the Wi-Fi firmware and old routers, the touch firmware and chargers, the modem firmware and the network. Our process treats vendor firmware updates much more lightly than our own code.

47 · Minji SeoSQE

I raised the same point in the storage review two weeks ago. A new firmware binary is a change, even if no line of our code moves. I'd like firmware updates to go through the same subsystem-tagged testing as code changes: a storage firmware triggers storage stress, a Wi-Fi firmware triggers the interoperability lab, and so on.

48 · Grace JungRelease Management

I agree, and I'll take it to the release council on Friday as a formal proposal, together with the rule for staged rollouts of security-related firmware. It's better to change the process now, while the examples are fresh, than after the next incident.
