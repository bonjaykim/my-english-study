Reallocating Quarterly Budget under Uncertain Demand

불확실한 수요 속 분기 예산 재배분

Disagreement and contingency planning

expense recognition

We will distinguish cash timing from expense recognition and separate commitments we can change from obligations we have already accepted.

Expense recognition differs from payment timing.

I have marked every line with its commitment status, but a few owners have not confirmed the details.

응용 연습
Check the commitment status before removing the line.

across-the-board cut

일률 삭감 · 동일 비율 삭감을 논의할 때

We are not approving an across-the-board cut, and the announcement should explain the reasons for the different adjustments.

응용 연습
An across-the-board cut may harm essential work.

contingency reserve

Would it be wiser to take the workshop reduction as well and hold twelve thousand in a contingency reserve?

응용 연습
The contingency reserve covers urgent repairs.

decision trail

응용 연습
Maintain a decision trail for each transfer.

double counting

이중 계산 · 같은 금액 중복 반영을 경계할 때

This prevents us from double counting the reserve as both a saving and spendable departmental money.

응용 연습
Double counting would overstate the savings.

net amount

순액 · 수수료 차감 후 실제 절감을 계산할 때

Report the net amount after cancellation fees.

protected audit preparation time

보호된 감사 준비 시간 · 필수 업무 시간을 확보할 때

응용 연습

variance report

차이 보고서 · 계획 대비 실적을 검토할 때

I will add a monthly variance report that separates timing differences from changes in total spending.

응용 연습

timing differences

시점 차이 · 지연 지급과 절감을 구분할 때

I will add a monthly variance report that separates timing differences from changes in total spending.

응용 연습
Timing differences do not change the total obligation.

impact assessment

The protected maintenance and support coverage are not exceptions obtained through negotiation skill; they follow our impact assessment.

Complete an impact assessment before cutting coverage.
