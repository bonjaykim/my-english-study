Second Week Status Review for Nova Tab after Launch

Overall Launch Health

The system crash rate is 0.12 per thousand device hours, which is lower than the previous tablet at the same point after launch. Wi-Fi disconnects are within normal range, so the configuration we pushed on day one is doing its job. The day-one update reached ninety-one percent of devices.

The Heat Fix

10 · Taeho LimKernel & BSP

The charging-aware thermal policy is complete and merged. It reduces the charging current when the charging corner sensor reads above forty degrees while the screen is on, and it adds the charging chip temperature to our telemetry.

11 · Nina ChoSQE

We're four days into the five-day validation. The full thermal matrix has passed so far, including the charging and video scenario at thirty-five degrees ambient. The hottest point on the back stays at or below forty-two point eight degrees in every scenario, compared with forty-six point two before the fix.

12 · Jaemin OhSQE

Charging time with the screen off is unchanged: fifty percent in twenty-nine minutes. With the screen on and video playing in a warm room, it's about forty-one minutes instead of twenty-nine. Most users will never notice the difference.

13 · Irene KangProgram Management

And the battery health regression?

14 · Nina ChoSQE

Clean. We ran an accelerated cycling test on ten devices, and the cell temperature during charging is now about four degrees lower on average, which should help battery longevity. The last day of validation is tomorrow, and I expect to sign off by Wednesday morning.

15 · Irene KangProgram Management

Then the Wi-Fi-only update stays on track for Thursday, September 3rd. Wait, I said the fourth last week. Let me check. Yes, Thursday the third. I'll correct the plan document.

16 · Eunji ParkCustomer Experience

Good catch. Our public statement said 'early September' without a specific date, so there's no conflict on our side.

New Issue: Keyboard Cover Disconnects

17 · Irene KangProgram Management

Let's move to the new issues. Eunji, what's new in the contact data?

18 · Eunji ParkCustomer Experience

Two new themes. The bigger one is the keyboard cover. Users report that it randomly stops working for a few seconds while they type, and sometimes they have to detach and reattach it. It's about twelve percent of contacts this week, up from almost nothing at launch, because more people are using the tablet for work now.

19 · Taeho LimKernel & BSP

That one landed with me on Friday. The keyboard cover connects through the magnetic pin connector on the side, and the driver for that connector uses a power-saving mode that suspends the link after two seconds of inactivity. When the user starts typing again, the link has to wake up, and on some units, the wake-up fails and the driver resets the connection. That's the few-second gap.

20 · Jaemin OhSQE

Do we know why the wake-up fails on some units and not others?

21 · Taeho LimKernel & BSP

Not yet. My current theory is contact resistance. The magnetic pins have small manufacturing variations, and on units with slightly higher resistance, the wake-up signal is weaker. The keyboard needs a few milliseconds longer to respond, and our driver's timeout is too tight. I've asked manufacturing for resistance measurements from a sample of returned units.

22 · Irene KangProgram Management

What's the fix, if your theory is right?

23 · Taeho LimKernel & BSP

Two options. Lengthen the wake-up timeout from five to twenty milliseconds, which costs nothing noticeable. Or disable the power-saving mode while the keyboard is attached and the screen is on. The second is more robust, but it costs a little battery, about one percent over a full day of typing. I'd start with the timeout and keep the second option in reserve.

New Issue: Stylus Latency

24 · Irene KangProgram Management

And the second new theme?

25 · Eunji ParkCustomer Experience

Stylus latency. A smaller number of users, mostly artists and students, say the pen feels laggy in our note-taking app compared with the previous tablet. Two popular creative reviewers made the same comment in their videos.

26 · Jaemin OhSQE

I measured it on Friday with a high-speed camera. In our note app, the time from pen contact to ink appearing on screen is about thirty-eight milliseconds, compared with twenty-six on the previous tablet. In third-party drawing apps, it's twenty-five. So the problem seems specific to our own app, not the hardware or the system.

27 · Irene KangProgram Management

That's helpful. It's not a system software issue, then. Who owns the note app?

28 · Jaemin OhSQE

The productivity apps team. I've sent them the measurements and the comparison with the third-party apps. Their initial guess is that a new handwriting recognition feature is processing strokes on the same thread that draws the ink. They're looking into it.

29 · Eunji ParkCustomer Experience

Can we tell users anything in the meantime? Creative users are very vocal, and they influence purchase decisions.

30 · Irene KangProgram Management

Let's wait until the app team confirms the cause before we say anything publicly. If their guess is right, the fix may be small, and we can announce it together with the update. I'll ask them for an estimate by Thursday.

Other Open Issues

31 · Irene KangProgram Management

Nina, what does the full open issue list look like?

32 · Nina ChoSQE

We have thirty-four open issues linked to the launch software. Zero critical, five major, and twenty-nine minor. The five majors are the heat issue, the keyboard cover, the stylus latency, a crash in split-screen mode when two specific apps are opened together, and an issue where the screen brightness briefly jumps when the device wakes up.

33 · Jaemin OhSQE

The split-screen crash is in one of the apps, not in the system. We've reported it to the developer through Lisa's team. The brightness jump is in the display framework; the fix is ready and small.

34 · Irene KangProgram Management

Returns and Business Impact

46 · Irene KangProgram Management

Before we close the issue review, I want to look at returns, because that's what the business will ask me about first. Eunji, do you have the numbers?

47 · Eunji ParkCustomer Experience

The return rate in the first eleven days is two point one percent, which is slightly better than the previous tablet's two point four. When customers give a reason, the most common one is simply that they changed their mind. Heat is mentioned in about six percent of returns, and the keyboard in about four percent. Nothing suggests a widespread quality problem.

48 · Jaemin OhSQE

That's reassuring. But the keyboard share could grow quickly as more people start using the tablet for work in September. If someone buys a tablet mainly for typing and the keyboard drops out every few minutes, that's a strong reason to return it.

49 · Irene KangProgram Management

50 · Taeho LimKernel & BSP

Within a day. Disabling the power-saving mode while the keyboard is attached is a simple condition in the driver. The cost is a little battery, which I'd accept as a temporary measure while we keep investigating the root cause.

51 · Irene KangProgram Management

Then whichever option is confirmed by Thursday goes into the monthly update. I'd rather ship a robust fix with a small battery cost than wait a month for the perfect one.

Lessons for the Next Tablet

52 · Nina ChoSQE

I'd like to note a few lessons now, while they're fresh, for the next tablet program. First, accessories like the keyboard cover need their own long-duration testing. We tested that the keyboard works, but not that it keeps working through hours of intermittent typing.

53 · Jaemin OhSQE

Second, pen latency should be a release metric with a target, measured on our own apps, not only on the system. We measured system-level latency carefully, and it was excellent. Nobody measured the note app, because it belongs to a different team.

54 · Taeho LimKernel & BSP

Third, manufacturing variation. The keyboard issue may come down to small differences in the magnetic pins, and our test units were all from the same early batch. We should always test with units from several production batches before launch, not just the first one.

55 · Irene KangProgram Management

These are good. I'll add them to the Nova Tab retrospective and share them with Mark, since Atlas Lite will have a similar keyboard accessory in some markets.

Telemetry Follow-Up

56 · Jaemin OhSQE

Once Thursday's update is out, we'll have the new charging chip temperature in telemetry for the first time. I'd like to compare the distribution in the first week with our chamber results, to confirm the lab matches the field.

57 · Taeho LimKernel & BSP

I'd also watch how often the charging limit activates. If it's more than a few percent of charging sessions, our threshold may be too conservative, and we could relax it slightly in October.

58 · Irene KangProgram Management

Agreed. Let's make both part of next week's review.

59 · Eunji ParkCustomer Experience

Could we also add the keyboard disconnect events to telemetry? Right now, we only know about the problem from contacts, which means we only hear from the users who bother to call. The real rate is probably several times higher.

60 · Taeho LimKernel & BSP

Good point. The driver already logs every link reset, so it's a small change to report them. I'll include it with the keyboard fix. Then, after the monthly update, we can see directly whether the resets drop to near zero, instead of waiting for contacts to go down.

61 · Jaemin OhSQE

And if we add it to telemetry for devices without the fix too, through the configuration service, we'd have a baseline before the update ships. That would make the before-and-after comparison much more convincing.

62 · Taeho LimKernel & BSP

Unfortunately, the reporting itself needs a driver change, so devices without the update won't send it. But the September update will reach most users within two weeks, so we'll still see a clear trend.
