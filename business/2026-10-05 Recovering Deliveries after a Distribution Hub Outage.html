Recovering Deliveries after a Distribution Hub Outage

물류 거점 장애 이후 배송 복구

가상의 유통기업이 분류기 고장으로 지연된 배송을 복구한다. 선입선출과 고객 영향 우선순위, 기록 불일치, 직원 과부하를 논의하고 물량 대조 후 영향 기반 복구를 결정한다. 모든 인물·회사·수치는 가상이다.

03 / Business expressions

outbound scan

출고 스캔 · 출발 확인 상태를 설명할 때

Our eastern hub lost its main sorting conveyor yesterday evening, leaving eight hundred parcels without a confirmed outbound scan.

응용 연습
The parcel needs an outbound scan.

physical reconciliation

실물 대조 · 시스템과 실제 수량을 맞출 때

We need a physical reconciliation before promising that every item is ready for manual dispatch.

응용 연습
Finish physical reconciliation before dispatch.

dispatch order

출고 순서 · 복구 물량 우선순위를 정할 때

Our purpose is to establish where the parcels are, decide a defensible dispatch order, and communicate realistic delivery expectations without creating additional confusion.

응용 연습
The dispatch order follows documented impact.

end to end

전체 과정에서 · 부분 속도와 전체 결과를 비교할 때

An option that looks faster inside the building may be slower end to end if it misses the final transport connection.

응용 연습
Measure performance end to end.

first in, first out

선입선출 · 도착 순서로 처리할 때

My initial preference was first in, first out because it is simple and avoids accusations of favoritism.

응용 연습
The ordinary queue uses first in, first out.

impact-based priority

영향 기반 우선순위 · 지연 결과로 순서를 정할 때

I suggest an impact-based priority within the confirmed inventory, using evidence of a fixed deadline or a documented interruption.

응용 연습
Use impact-based priority for the recovery queue.

incident register

장애 대장 · 복구 결정을 추적할 때

Yes, we can add a recovery classification to the incident register while leaving the order record unchanged.

응용 연습
Record the exception in the incident register.

last-minute resequencing

막판 순서 재배열 · 처리 중 잦은 변경을 제한할 때

I support the classification, but I need a limit on last-minute resequencing.

응용 연습
Last-minute resequencing increases error risk.

operational cost

Diego can approve exceptions, but each should record the operational cost.

manual handling method

수동 취급 방법 · 수작업 안전과 절차를 정할 때

We will use the approved manual handling method and stop a task if the parcel weight or equipment condition makes it unsuitable.

응용 연습
Follow the approved manual handling method.

controlled test run

통제된 시험 가동 · 설비 재개를 검증할 때

The conveyor will return to service only after the equipment team completes its checks and a controlled test run succeeds.

확인된 출발 · 실제 복구 진전을 측정할 때

The first recovery measure will be confirmed departures, not parcels moved between holding areas.

Report confirmed departures rather than floor movements.
