Each team will report status, then we'll go through the open issues, and we'll finish with a go or no-go recommendation that I'll take to the release council tomorrow morning.

02 · Minji SeoSQE

Minji, SQE. Tom is with me; he ran the stress campaigns on the candidate build.

03 · Jiwoo LeeKernel & BSP

Jiwoo and Hyunsoo from Kernel and BSP. We'll cover the storage fix, and Hyunsoo has the power numbers.

Kernel Storage Fix Status

06 · Jiwoo LeeKernel & BSP

We have a confirmed root cause and a verified fix. The four-group experiment we designed last Monday gave us very clean results. The control group, running the unchanged 2.1.0 build, had six panics on sixty devices. The group with the original five-hundred-millisecond timeout had zero. The group with the old storage firmware also had zero. And the debug group showed exactly the race we suspected: in nine separate events, a late completion arrived after the error handler had already aborted and released the request.

07 · Grace JungRelease Management

So both layers of the hypothesis were confirmed. What did we actually change in 2.1.1?

08 · Jiwoo LeeKernel & BSP

Two changes. First, the suspend timeout goes to one second, which matches the Qorvix reference driver and the storage specification. Second, the completion path now checks whether the request is still valid before touching it. If it isn't, we log a warning and ignore the completion instead of crashing. Laura from Qorvix reviewed both changes, and she confirmed the register dumps show the controller behaving within specification. So this was purely a software issue on our side.

09 · Tom AhnSQE

And the stress results on the 2.1.1 candidate are clean. We ran the full seventy-two-hour profile on two hundred devices, and we had zero storage panics. We did see the validity check fire three times in the logs, which tells us the late completions still happen occasionally, but now they're harmless.

10 · Grace JungRelease Management

That's exactly the kind of evidence I like to see: not just the crash going away, but proof that the protection is doing its job.

11 · Hyunsoo BaeKernel & BSP

On the battery side, since the shorter timeout was originally introduced to save standby power, I measured the impact of going to one second. Overnight standby drain went from 2.6 percent to 3.0 percent on our reference devices. That's a small regression compared to 2.1.0, but it's still better than 2.0.3, which was at 3.2 percent. I think it's acceptable for now, and I'll look for a smarter way to save that power later.

12 · Minji SeoSQE

From SQE's perspective, that trade-off is clearly right. Nobody complains about a 0.4 percent difference overnight. Everyone complains about a crash.

Overall Issue Status

13 · Grace JungRelease Management

Good. Minji, can you give us the overall picture of open issues on the 2.1.1 candidate?

14 · Minji SeoSQE

Sure. We have twenty-three open issues found on 2.1.1. Zero are critical, two are major, and twenty-one are minor. Of the twenty-one minor ones, eighteen also exist on the current production build, 2.0.3, so they're not regressions. The other three are cosmetic issues in the settings app, and the framework team has fixes queued for 2.1.2.

15 · Grace JungRelease Management

And the two major issues?

16 · Tom AhnSQE

The first one is the display driver watchdog bite that showed up alongside the storage panics last week. We've seen it twice more since then, so three times in roughly fourteen thousand device hours. It also reproduces on 2.0.3 at a similar rate, so it's not new. The display team believes it's related to a rare timing issue during panel power-off, and they're still collecting data.

17 · Tom AhnSQE

The second major issue is the out-of-memory panic, which we've seen once on 2.1.1 and twice on 2.0.3, always after more than sixty hours of continuous stress with dozens of apps open. It's real, but it's hard to imagine a normal user hitting that pattern. Neither of the major issues is a regression, which is the key point for this decision.

18 · Grace JungRelease Management

Agreed. Our rule for maintenance releases is that we don't ship new critical or major regressions, and we don't make existing issues worse. It sounds like 2.1.1 meets both conditions.

The Camera Hang Question

19 · Grace JungRelease Management

Now the harder question. Yesterday's triage on the camera recording hang produced a timeout fix. Should it go into 2.1.1, or wait for the next build?

20 · Tom AhnSQE

Here's where we are. Junho delivered the test build this morning. Soyeon started error injection testing an hour ago, and early results are very encouraging: forty injections so far, zero system hangs. But the full validation plan we agreed on finishes on Monday, August 17th, with the camera regression suite.

21 · Minji SeoSQE

So if we include it, 2.1.1 can't be finalized until the seventeenth at the earliest. If we don't include it, 2.1.1 is ready today.

22 · Jiwoo LeeKernel & BSP

My instinct is not to hold the storage fix hostage to the camera fix. Every day 2.1.1 is delayed, devices on 2.0.3 keep running with the old storage driver. To be fair, 2.0.3 doesn't have the panic, but it also doesn't have the dozens of other fixes that were in 2.1.0, including two security fixes from July.

23 · Tom AhnSQE

On the other hand, the camera hang is the more visible issue for users right now. It's been reported by a reviewer, and the video of the frozen concert recording has a few hundred thousand views. If we ship a maintenance release that doesn't address it, some people will ask why.

24 · Grace JungRelease Management

That's a fair concern, but I'd rather answer 'it's coming in the next update' than rush a fix into a build that has to go to forty carriers. Is there any reason the camera fix couldn't go into the September build, which has a content cutoff on August 25th?

25 · Minji SeoSQE

No, none. Validation finishes on the seventeenth, which leaves more than a week of margin before the September cutoff. And September is already planned as a security-focused build, so a small, well-tested driver fix fits there nicely.

26 · Tom AhnSQE

Okay, I'm persuaded. As long as we commit publicly to a timeframe, users can live with a few more weeks. Most people don't record forty-minute 4K videos every day.

27 · Grace JungRelease Management

Then let's agree on that. The camera fix goes into the September build. In the meantime, could we publish a short tip suggesting users record long videos in 4K thirty frames per second until the update arrives?

28 · Minji SeoSQE

That's a sensible workaround. I'll suggest it to the support team, along with the data showing 4K thirty is unaffected even after two hours.

Modem Software Update

29 · Grace JungRelease Management

It's not ready for 2.1.1, and I wouldn't want it there anyway, because a modem firmware change needs carrier approval, which would slow down the whole build.

Rollout Plan

37 · Grace JungRelease Management

Let's talk about the rollout for 2.1.1. Since 2.1.0 never went out, we're essentially doing the original rollout with a fixed build. I propose we submit to the carriers that require approval tomorrow, and start the open-market rollout on Tuesday, August 18th: one percent for forty-eight hours, then ten percent, then everyone by August 25th.

38 · Tom AhnSQE

I'd like the halt criteria to include the storage-specific signal, not just the overall crash rate. If we see any storage panic signature in the one percent group, we stop, even if the overall rate looks fine.

39 · Jiwoo LeeKernel & BSP

I'd add the validity check warning as a metric too. If it fires much more often in the field than in the lab, it doesn't mean users are affected, but it would tell us the timing in the real world is different from what we tested, and I'd want to know that early.

40 · Grace JungRelease Management

Good, both of those go into the rollout plan. Hyunsoo, anything on power we should watch?

41 · Hyunsoo BaeKernel & BSP

Just the standby drain metric from telemetry. If it goes up by more than half a percent overnight compared to 2.0.3 devices, I'd want to look before we expand. I don't expect it, but it's cheap to watch.

Process Lessons from the Hold

42 · Grace JungRelease Management

Before we wrap up, I'd like to spend five minutes on process, because the council will certainly ask how a panic like this reached a release candidate in the first place. What would have caught it earlier?

43 · Hyunsoo BaeKernel & BSP

Honestly, the timeout change should have been tested together with the new storage firmware. They were merged a week apart, by different teams, and each was tested on its own. Nobody owned the combination. If we had a rule that any change to storage power management triggers a run of the storage stress profile with the latest firmware, we would have seen it two weeks before the release candidate.

44 · Tom AhnSQE

From the lab side, we could make that automatic. We already tag changes by subsystem. If a change touches the storage driver, we can schedule a smaller, twelve-hour storage stress run on fifty devices overnight. It wouldn't catch everything, but at a five percent panic rate, it would almost certainly have caught this one.

45 · Minji SeoSQE

I like that, and I'd extend the same idea to firmware updates from vendors. A new firmware binary is a change too, even if no line of our code moves. Right now, firmware updates go through a much lighter process than code changes, and that's a gap.

46 · Jiwoo LeeKernel & BSP

Agreed. I'll write up both proposals in the root cause summary, so the council sees concrete prevention steps, not just the fix.

47 · Grace JungRelease Management

Perfect. The council always responds better to 'here's what we changed' than to 'here's what went wrong'.

48 · Grace JungRelease Management

The camera hang fix and the modem firmware go into the September build. Carrier submissions tomorrow, open-market rollout starting August 18th with the halt criteria we discussed. Does anyone disagree?

49 · Minji SeoSQE

No disagreement. SQE signs off on 2.1.1.

Hyunsoo, add the standby drain comparison to the dashboard. Seojin, keep the firmware validation on track for September. And I'll prepare the September content list by Friday.

커널 스토리지 수정 현황

06 · Jiwoo LeeKernel & BSP

두 가지를 바꿨습니다. 첫째, 서스펜드 타임아웃을 1초로 했는데, Qorvix 레퍼런스 드라이버와 스토리지 규격에 맞춘 겁니다. 둘째, 완료 경로가 이제 요청을 건드리기 전에 아직 유효한지 확인합니다. 유효하지 않으면 크래시를 내는 대신 경고를 로그로 남기고 그 완료를 무시합니다. Qorvix의 Laura가 두 변경 모두 리뷰했고, 레지스터 덤프상 컨트롤러가 규격대로 동작했다고 확인해 줬습니다. 그러니 이건 순전히 저희 쪽 소프트웨어 문제였습니다.

그리고 2.1.1 후보 빌드의 스트레스 결과는 깨끗합니다. 200대에서 72시간 전체 프로필을 돌렸고 스토리지 패닉은 0건이었습니다. 로그에서 유효성 검사가 세 번 동작한 게 보였는데, 늦은 완료가 여전히 가끔 발생하지만 이제는 무해하다는 뜻입니다.
