Agenda and Overall Numbers

08 · Minji SeoSQE

Thanks, Grace. Orion Lite 1.3.0 went out to the first market wave on July 28th, so we have one week of data. Right now we have forty-seven open issues linked to this release: three critical, twelve major, and thirty-two minor. That's up from thirty-one issues at the end of last week, so the trend is still going the wrong way, but the rate of new issues is slowing down.

09 · Grace JungRelease Management

How many of the forty-seven are actually new regressions in 1.3.0, as opposed to old issues that we just happened to notice?

10 · Minji SeoSQE

11 · Minji SeoSQE

In terms of field data, the crash rate for system processes went from 0.21 to 0.34 per thousand device hours after the update. Most of that increase comes from one issue, the camera ANR, which Elena will cover. Battery complaints in the user feedback channel also doubled, which lines up with the standby drain issue Yuna is looking at.

Platform Framework Update

12 · Grace JungRelease Management

Let's start with the biggest contributor, then. Elena, where are we on the camera ANR?

13 · Elena ParkPlatform Framework

We've made good progress. The ANR happens when the camera app is launched cold within a few seconds of the device unlocking. The camera service waits on a lock in the system server that's held by the new face unlock feature during its cleanup. On most devices it's released quickly, but on devices with a lot of installed apps, cleanup takes over five seconds, and the camera app hits the ANR timeout.

14 · Grace JungRelease Management

Do we have a fix?

15 · Elena ParkPlatform Framework

We have a fix in code review. It moves the cleanup off the critical path, onto a background thread, so it no longer holds the lock while it iterates over packages. We've tested it on fifty devices with no ANRs so far. I'd like two more days of soak testing before I call it done.

16 · Minji SeoSQE

I want to challenge the classification, though. Your team marked this as major, but it's the single biggest driver of our crash rate increase, and it's visible to every user who opens the camera after unlocking. From the SQE side, I'd call it critical.

17 · Brian ShinPlatform Framework

I see where you're coming from, Minji. Our reasoning was that the app recovers after the ANR dialog, and no data is lost. Under our severity guidelines, that's major. But I take the point about user visibility. I don't feel strongly about the label, as long as it doesn't change the priority, because we're already treating it as our top item.

18 · Grace JungRelease Management

Let's upgrade it to critical, then, mainly for reporting purposes, so management sees it at the top of the list. Brian, any other framework items in the top tier?

19 · Brian ShinPlatform Framework

One more. Bluetooth audio stutters with a specific family of third-party earbuds. We've traced it to a codec negotiation change in the Bluetooth stack. The stack is from our connectivity vendor, so we've sent them logs, and they've acknowledged the issue. Their estimate for a fix is August 12th, which is later than I'd like.

20 · Grace JungRelease Management

Is there a workaround on our side, in case the vendor fix slips?

21 · Brian ShinPlatform Framework

There is a fallback. We can add those earbuds to a compatibility list that forces the older codec. Audio quality is slightly lower, but there's no stuttering. It's a one-line configuration change, and we can remove it once the real fix lands.

Kernel and BSP Update

22 · Grace JungRelease Management

Good to have that in our back pocket. Let's move to kernel. Chris, Yuna?

23 · Yuna KwakKernel & BSP

I'll start with standby battery drain, since it's the most visible. After the update, some devices lose around eight percent overnight instead of the usual three. Our wakelock analysis shows the location service waking the device every ninety seconds when the user has a specific fitness app installed. The app didn't change, but the framework changed how location requests are batched in 1.3.0.

24 · Brian ShinPlatform Framework

Wait, so this is actually a framework change? I thought it was assigned to kernel because of the wakelocks.

25 · Yuna KwakKernel & BSP

It started with us because the symptom showed up in the kernel wakeup sources. But the root cause is the batching change in the location manager. I sent the analysis to your team on Friday, but I'm not sure anyone picked it up.

26 · Brian ShinPlatform Framework

That's on me. It probably fell through the cracks between the two queues. Let's reassign it to framework today, with you as a reviewer, Yuna, since you know the wakeup side better than anyone.

27 · Grace JungRelease Management

Thank you both. This is exactly why we have this meeting. Chris, what else is on the kernel list?

28 · Chris MoonKernel & BSP

The third critical issue is a fingerprint unlock latency regression. Median unlock time went from about three hundred milliseconds to five hundred and fifty. We bisected it to a scheduler tuning change that was meant to save power by keeping small tasks on the efficiency cores. The fingerprint processing thread now starts on a slow core and takes too long to migrate.

29 · Minji SeoSQE

Is the fix to revert the tuning, or to make an exception for the fingerprint path?

30 · Chris MoonKernel & BSP

We'd prefer a targeted fix: we boost the fingerprint thread when a touch event lands on the sensor area, so it starts on a performance core. Reverting the whole tuning would undo a four percent standby power improvement. The targeted fix is ready and tested on twenty devices. Unlock time is back to three hundred and ten milliseconds.

31 · Minji SeoSQE

Twenty devices is a small sample for a scheduler change. Before it goes into a hotfix, I'd want at least our standard performance regression suite on a hundred devices, because scheduler tweaks tend to have side effects in places you don't expect.

32 · Chris MoonKernel & BSP

That's reasonable. If SQE can take the build tomorrow, the suite takes about two days, so we'd have results by Thursday.

Unclassified Issues and Field Signals

33 · Grace JungRelease Management

35 · Chris MoonKernel & BSP

36 · Minji SeoSQE

Unfortunately, the dogfood device had ramdump collection disabled, because it's someone's daily phone. We only have the reset reason, which says it was a hardware watchdog. I've asked the owner to enable collection, but he's not thrilled about it.

37 · Yuna KwakKernel & BSP

A hardware watchdog on its own could be many things, but it's worth correlating with the heat reports from users. If the device was thermally throttled hard enough, some threads might have been starved long enough to miss the watchdog kick. I can check whether the thermal logs from those four feedback reports show anything unusual.

38 · Grace JungRelease Management

39 · Minji SeoSQE

I'm fine with two weeks, as long as the reboot and heat reports are excluded from that rule until Yuna's analysis is done. If there's a thermal pattern, it could become our next critical issue, and I don't want to close it prematurely.

40 · Brian ShinPlatform Framework

On the field side, one more signal to watch: the app store reviews for our camera app dropped from 4.3 to 4.1 stars in a week. Most of the negative reviews mention the freeze after unlocking. Once the ANR fix is out, that's a good metric to confirm we've actually solved it for users.

Cross-Program Update
