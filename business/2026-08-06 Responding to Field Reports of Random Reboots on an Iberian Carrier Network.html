Responding to Field Reports of Random Reboots on an Iberian Carrier Network

이베리아 통신망에서 보고된 무작위 재부팅 시장 이슈 대응

스페인과 포르투갈의 한 통신사 망에서 Orion 사용자들의 무작위 재부팅 신고가 급증했습니다. 시장 품질, 사내 모뎀 소프트웨어, 칩셋 벤더 Qorvix 모뎀 팀, 유럽 통신사 엔지니어링 팀이 텔레메트리 분석 결과와 단기·중기 대응 방안을 논의합니다.

Field Data Overview

07 · Hannah OhField Quality

Thanks, everyone. Kyle, could you start us off with what the field data shows?

08 · Kyle JangField Quality

Sure. Starting on July 30th, the unexpected reboot rate for Orion devices on this carrier's network in Spain and Portugal jumped from our normal baseline of about 0.03 percent of devices per day to 0.9 percent per day. That's a thirty-fold increase. On every other carrier in Europe, the rate is flat. And the same Orion software on the same carrier's network in other countries is also flat. So this is very specific to one carrier group in one region.

09 · Hannah OhField Quality

And did anything change on our side around July 30th? An OTA, a configuration push?

10 · Kyle JangField Quality

Nothing. The last Orion OTA in that region was on July 9th, and the reboot rate stayed flat for three weeks after it. We also haven't pushed any server-side configuration changes since mid-July. That's what makes us think the trigger is on the network side.

11 · Hannah OhField Quality

How is this showing up for customers? I've seen some noise on social media, but I'd like the full picture.

12 · Kyle JangField Quality

Customer contacts about random restarts in Spain are up about four times compared to last month. Most users say the phone restarts while they're on the move, often on the train or in the car. A few mention it happening during calls, which is obviously the worst moment. There are two threads on a popular Spanish tech forum with several hundred replies, and one tech news site picked it up yesterday.

Technical Analysis

13 · Hannah OhField Quality

Seojin, what do the crash logs tell us?

14 · Seojin LimModem Software

The reboots are all the same type: a modem subsystem crash that escalates into a full device restart. Normally, when the modem firmware crashes, the application processor restarts only the modem, and the user sees a few seconds without signal. But on Orion, for this carrier's software configuration, the policy is set to trigger a full system restart with a ramdump instead. That policy was requested during the carrier's acceptance testing last year, so they could collect complete dumps. It was supposed to be reverted before launch, and it wasn't.

15 · Hannah OhField Quality

So there are really two problems here: the modem is crashing, and when it crashes, we take down the whole phone instead of just restarting the modem.

16 · Seojin LimModem Software

Exactly. And the second problem is entirely on us. That's the frustrating part, because it turned a minor, recoverable glitch into a very visible reboot.

17 · Wei ZhangQorvix Modem Engineering

On the first problem, I've analyzed twelve of the modem dumps Pedro collected. All twelve show the same assertion in the handover module. It happens during a handover from a 5G cell to an LTE cell, when the network sends a measurement configuration with a combination of parameters our firmware didn't expect. Specifically, the configuration includes a timer value that's outside the range in our parameter table, and the firmware asserts instead of clamping the value.

18 · Lucia FernandezEurope Carrier Engineering

That fits what the carrier told me. On July 29th, they rolled out a network optimization across their 5G sites in Spain and Portugal, which changed handover parameters to reduce dropped calls in high-speed scenarios, like trains and highways. They say it's fully compliant with the standard, and other device makers haven't reported problems.

19 · Wei ZhangQorvix Modem Engineering

I believe them that it's compliant. The value is unusual, but it's valid according to the specification. The bug is in our firmware. We should handle any valid value gracefully, and we didn't. I'll be straightforward about that.

20 · Pedro AlvesEurope Carrier Engineering

That also explains the pattern in my test drives. On the Lisbon to Porto train, I reproduced the reboot four times in three hours, always within a few seconds of the phone switching from 5G to LTE. In the city, where handovers happen at lower speed, I couldn't reproduce it at all in two days.

Response Options

21 · Hannah OhField Quality

So we have a clear root cause, which is great. Now let's talk about how to get relief to customers as fast as possible. I see three levers: ask the carrier to roll back the parameter change, fix our restart policy, and fix the modem firmware. Let's go through them in order of speed.

22 · Lucia FernandezEurope Carrier Engineering

I'll be realistic about the first option. I already asked informally yesterday, and they were not enthusiastic. The change reduced dropped calls on their high-speed routes by almost twenty percent, and they don't want to roll back an improvement for all their customers because of a bug in one device. I understand their position. We might get a partial rollback on specific train routes as a goodwill gesture, but I wouldn't count on it.

23 · Hannah OhField Quality

Fair enough. We shouldn't ask them to fix our bug. Seojin, how fast can we change the restart policy?

24 · Seojin LimModem Software

This is the good news. The policy is controlled by a configuration flag that we can update through our device configuration service, without a full OTA. If we flip it, a modem crash restarts only the modem. The user loses signal for about five seconds instead of losing the whole phone for a minute. Technically, we could push it to the affected devices within twenty-four hours.

25 · Kyle JangField Quality

I want to raise one concern about that. If we flip the flag, the reboots disappear from our telemetry, but the modem crashes don't. We'd lose visibility of the real problem, and a five-second signal loss during a call still drops the call. I don't want us to declare victory because the dashboard turns green.

26 · Seojin LimModem Software

That's a very good point. We can keep visibility by reporting modem subsystem restarts as a separate telemetry event. The pipeline already supports it; we just haven't been watching that metric for this carrier. And you're right that it's a mitigation, not a fix.

27 · Hannah OhField Quality

Then the real fix is the firmware. Wei, what's the timeline?

28 · Wei ZhangQorvix Modem Engineering

The code change itself is small: we clamp the timer value to the supported range and log a warning instead of asserting. My team can deliver a patched firmware to Seojin's team by Monday, August 10th. But firmware changes in the handover module need regression testing on our side across the major network vendors' equipment, which normally takes about a week. So a fully validated firmware would be ready around August 14th.

29 · Seojin LimModem Software

Then on our side, the firmware has to go into an Orion OTA, which means our own regression plus carrier approval. Lucia, how long does this carrier usually take to approve a maintenance OTA?

30 · Lucia FernandezEurope Carrier Engineering

Normally about ten working days. But given the customer impact, and the fact that it's a modem fix for their network, I think I can get them to fast-track it to five. They want this fixed as much as we do, because their store staff are getting the complaints too.

Exposure on Other Products

31 · Hannah OhField Quality

Yes, unfortunately. The same module is used in the firmware for Orion Lite and for the Orion Fold, which hasn't launched yet. The parameter table is identical, so in theory, both are vulnerable to the same configuration. I'd recommend we include them in the fix rather than wait to see crashes.

35 · Hannah OhField Quality

36 · Seojin LimModem Software

For Orion Fold, yes, because it's still in development and the fix can ride along with the next firmware drop before launch. For Orion Lite, it would go into the September maintenance release. It's a lower priority there, since nobody is affected today, but I'd still like it in before the carrier expands the rollout.

37 · Pedro AlvesEurope Carrier Engineering

I'll ask the carrier's radio team whether they have a date for the low-band rollout. If they plan it for October, the September release gives us enough margin. If it's earlier, we'll need to rethink.

Preventing a Repeat

43 · Kyle JangField Quality

44 · Seojin LimModem Software

I checked that last night. It was added to the carrier-specific configuration file during acceptance testing, with a comment saying 'remove before launch'. There was no automated check for debug settings in carrier configurations, only in the main build configuration. I've already scanned all the other carrier configurations for Orion and Orion Lite, and this is the only one with that setting.

45 · Hannah OhField Quality

That's a relief. Let's make sure it can't happen again. Can we extend the build check to cover carrier configurations, so a debug-only setting fails the release build?

46 · Seojin LimModem Software

Yes. It's a straightforward change to the release checks. I'll have it in place by the end of next week, and I'll present it at the release council so other programs adopt it too.

03 / Business expressions

in the same room

한자리에 모여 · 관련된 모든 팀이 함께 논의해야 할 때

We have a field issue that's growing fast in one region, and I want to make sure every team that can help is in the same room.

응용 연습
Let's get the vendor and SQE in the same room before Friday.

start us off

(논의를) 시작해 주다 · 회의의 첫 발표를 요청할 때

Kyle, could you start us off with what the field data shows?

응용 연습
Could you start us off with the crash numbers from last night?

thirty-fold increase

30배 증가 · 수치의 급격한 증가를 배수로 표현할 때

That's a thirty-fold increase.

응용 연습
We saw a ten-fold increase in ANRs after the update.

the full picture

전체 그림, 전반적인 상황 · 부분적인 정보가 아닌 전체 상황을 알고 싶을 때

I've seen some noise on social media, but I'd like the full picture.

응용 연습
Before we escalate, I'd like the full picture from all three regions.

picked it up

(뉴스 등이) 다루다, 보도하다 · 언론이나 커뮤니티가 이슈를 다루기 시작했을 때

There are two threads on a popular Spanish tech forum with several hundred replies, and one tech news site picked it up yesterday.

응용 연습
A tech blog picked it up within an hour of the first report.

entirely on us

전적으로 우리 책임인 · 자기 팀의 책임을 분명히 인정할 때

And the second problem is entirely on us.

응용 연습
Missing the config review is entirely on us.

I'll be straightforward about that

그 점은 솔직히 인정하겠다 · 자사의 잘못을 숨기지 않고 말할 때

I'll be straightforward about that.

응용 연습
Our test coverage missed this case, and I'll be straightforward about that.

get relief to customers

고객에게 해결책(완화책)을 전달하다 · 근본 수정 전이라도 고객 고통을 빨리 줄이자고 할 때

Now let's talk about how to get relief to customers as fast as possible.

응용 연습
A server-side flag is the fastest way to get relief to customers.

I wouldn't count on it

기대하지 않는 게 좋겠다 · 가능성이 낮은 일을 현실적으로 말할 때

We might get a partial rollback on specific train routes as a goodwill gesture, but I wouldn't count on it.

응용 연습
The vendor might deliver early, but I wouldn't count on it.

declare victory

승리를 선언하다, 성급히 해결됐다고 하다 · 표면적인 개선만 보고 문제가 해결됐다고 판단하지 말자고 할 때

I don't want us to declare victory because the dashboard turns green.

응용 연습
Let's not declare victory until the field data is stable for a week.

a mitigation, not a fix

해결책이 아니라 완화책 · 임시 조치와 근본 수정을 구분할 때

And you're right that it's a mitigation, not a fix.

응용 연습
Disabling the feature is a mitigation, not a fix.

fast-track

신속 처리하다 · 승인이나 절차를 앞당겨 달라고 할 때

But given the customer impact, and the fact that it's a modem fix for their network, I think I can get them to fast-track it to five.

응용 연습
Can we fast-track the security patch through certification?

go a long way

큰 도움이 되다, 큰 효과가 있다 · 작은 행동이 큰 효과를 낼 것이라고 말할 때

응용 연습
A clear known issues list would go a long way with the carrier.

as a courtesy

예의상, 배려 차원에서 · 의무는 아니지만 관계를 위해 미리 공유할 때

I'll also share the draft statement with the carrier before it goes out, as a courtesy.

응용 연습
As a courtesy, let's notify the vendor before we publish the bug.

That's a relief

다행이네요 · 우려했던 상황이 아님을 확인했을 때

That's a relief.

응용 연습
That's a relief; the other models aren't affected.

report back

결과를 보고하다 · 조사나 테스트 후 결과를 다시 알려 주겠다고 할 때

I'll take the 8 a.m. train tomorrow and report back by the afternoon.

응용 연습
Run the test overnight and report back in the morning.
