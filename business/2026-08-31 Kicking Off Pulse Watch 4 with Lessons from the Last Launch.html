Kicking Off Pulse Watch 4 with Lessons from the Last Launch

지난 출시의 교훈을 반영한 Pulse Watch 4 킥오프

내년 8월 출시 예정인 Pulse Watch 4의 소프트웨어 킥오프 회의입니다. 프로그램 관리, 플랫폼 프레임워크, 커널·BSP, SQE 팀이 Pulse Watch 3 출시에서 얻은 교훈을 바탕으로 새 칩셋 일정, 배터리 목표 설정 방식, 출시 당일 업데이트 계획, 약한 신호와 특이 부팅 테스트, 마일스톤을 합의합니다.

Opening

Good afternoon, everyone. Welcome to the kickoff for Pulse Watch 4. Most of you worked on Pulse Watch 3, which launched just over two weeks ago, so the lessons are still fresh. I've asked everyone to keep the retrospective notes open, because I want this program to start with those lessons built in, not discover them again next summer.

02 · Alex YooPlatform Framework

Alex, Platform Framework. I'll lead connectivity again, and I'm very motivated to get Bluetooth right from the start this time.

03 · Mina HwangPlatform Framework

Mina, also Framework. Boot, recovery, and setup flow.

04 · Hoon JeongKernel & BSP

Hoon Jeong, Kernel and BSP. I'm new to the watch team; I moved over from the Orion Lite kernel team last week. Dongwook is on Atlas Lite now, so I'll be the kernel lead for Pulse Watch 4.

05 · Nari ChoiSQE

Nari, SQE. I found the boot loop on Pulse Watch 3, so I'll be watching very closely.

06 · Jason KwonSQE

Jason, SQE lead for wearables. Welcome to the team, Hoon.

Product Background

07 · Sarah LimProgram Management

Let me give the background. Pulse Watch 4 targets a retail launch on August 13th, 2027. It moves to Qorvix's new wearable chip, the W5, which is about forty percent more power-efficient than the current one. It adds a new optical health sensor and a larger battery. And it will ship on the next wearable platform version from Google.

08 · Hoon JeongKernel & BSP

A new chip is the biggest risk from the kernel side. When does the first W5 BSP arrive?

09 · Sarah LimProgram Management

Qorvix has committed to October 9th for the first drop, with an evaluation board available from mid-September. Laura from Qorvix confirmed the dates in writing last Friday.

10 · Hoon JeongKernel & BSP

Good. If the evaluation board is available in mid-September, I'd like two of them immediately. Based on what Dongwook did on Atlas Lite, starting early on an evaluation board saves about a month later.

11 · Sarah LimProgram Management

I'll request them today, and I'll ask Laura whether Qorvix can include a sample of their power measurements on the evaluation board, so we have a baseline before our own hardware arrives.

Lesson One: The Battery Claim

12 · Sarah LimProgram Management

Let's go through the lessons one by one and turn each into a concrete decision. Lesson one: the battery claim. On Pulse Watch 3, marketing claimed forty hours, measured with the always-on display off, while most users had it on and got about thirty. Jason, how do we avoid that?

13 · Jason KwonSQE

SQE should define the battery test profile together with marketing, before any number is published, and the profile must use the default settings that ship on the device. If the always-on display is on by default, the claim must be measured with it on. We'll also publish the profile internally, so everyone knows exactly what the number means.

14 · Mina HwangPlatform Framework

And we should decide the default early. The setup screen we added in the September update, where users choose between always-on and standard display, worked well. For Pulse Watch 4, I'd build that choice into setup from the start.

15 · Sarah LimProgram Management

Then the decision is: battery claims use default settings, the test profile is agreed with marketing by the end of the year, and the always-on choice is part of setup. I'll get marketing's agreement this week, before anyone writes a number in a presentation.

16 · Jason KwonSQE

One more thing: a forty percent more efficient chip will tempt marketing to promise a very big number. I'd ask that the claim isn't set until we've measured it on real hardware with real software, which won't be before February.

Lesson Two: Real-World Connectivity

17 · Sarah LimProgram Management

Lesson two: our test lab was too friendly for Bluetooth. Alex?

18 · Alex YooPlatform Framework

On Pulse Watch 3, every test watch sat next to its phone with a perfect signal, so we never saw the reconnect storm that drained batteries and delayed notifications. For Pulse Watch 4, the reconnect policy we shipped in September becomes the baseline, and every nightly build runs the shielded box test with weak and fluctuating signals.

19 · Nari ChoiSQE

I'd extend it beyond Bluetooth. Wi-Fi with old routers, which caused problems on Orion, and LTE in areas with poor coverage. Wearables spend a lot of time in pockets and under sleeves, so weak signals are normal, not an edge case.

20 · Alex YooPlatform Framework

Agreed. I'll ask Haeun's team whether we can borrow some of the old routers they're adding to the interoperability lab. There's no point in building our own collection if theirs already reflects real customer homes.

21 · Sarah LimProgram Management

Good. That's decision two: weak-signal testing for Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and LTE in the nightly suite from the first booting build.

Lesson Three: Unusual Boots

22 · Sarah LimProgram Management

Lesson three: the boot loop. Mina, Nari?

23 · Mina HwangPlatform Framework

The root cause was a provisioning service that couldn't handle a slow modem, hidden for years by a wait in the boot sequence. The engineering lesson, from Dongwook's tech share, is that we never remove an ordering constraint without understanding why it exists. For Pulse Watch 4, I'd like every service that depends on the modem to handle the modem not being ready, explicitly, with tests.

24 · Nari ChoiSQE

And on the testing side: the unusual boot scenarios go into the nightly suite from the first build. Factory reset with an active eSIM, boot after an update, boot with full storage, boot after a crash, and first boot in a region without network coverage. On Pulse Watch 3, we only found the loop two weeks before launch because we only tested the normal boot.

25 · Hoon JeongKernel & BSP

From the kernel side, I'll adopt the per-phase boot budget that Dongwook proposed for Atlas. Watches are more sensitive to boot time than phones, because users restart them more often to fix problems. And I'll document every ordering dependency between the modem and the rest of the system on day one.

26 · Sarah LimProgram Management

Excellent. Decision three: unusual boot scenarios in the nightly suite from the first build, explicit modem readiness handling, and a per-phase boot budget.

Lesson Four: Planning the Day-One Update

27 · Sarah LimProgram Management

Lesson four: the day-one update. On Pulse Watch 3, it saved the launch, but we planned it in a panic. This time, I'd like it in the schedule from the start.

28 · Mina HwangPlatform Framework

I'd go further. The setup flow should always offer the update over the phone's connection if Wi-Fi is skipped. On Pulse Watch 3, update adoption in some European markets was only sixty percent because people skipped Wi-Fi. That one design choice would have protected thousands more watches.

29 · Jason KwonSQE

Planning a day-one update shouldn't become an excuse to ship a weaker factory build, though. I'd like a rule: the factory build must meet all launch criteria on its own. The day-one update is for improvements and late fixes, not for things we knew were broken.

30 · Sarah LimProgram Management

I strongly agree with that. Decision four: a day-one update is planned in the schedule, setup always offers it, and the factory build must meet launch criteria by itself.

Lesson Five: Involving Support Early

31 · Sarah LimProgram Management

Lesson five came from Eunji in Customer Experience. Her team learned about the boot loop recovery method on launch day. She asked to be involved much earlier this time.

32 · Jason KwonSQE

I'd invite Customer Experience to the monthly status reviews from feature complete onward. They don't need every technical detail, but they should see the known issues list, so they can prepare recovery steps and answers before customers need them.

33 · Alex YooPlatform Framework

They also see patterns we don't. On Pulse Watch 3, their contact data was the first clear signal of the battery and notification problems, before our telemetry dashboards made it obvious.

34 · Sarah LimProgram Management

Decision five, then: Customer Experience joins monthly reviews from feature complete. I'll invite Eunji today.

The New Health Sensor

35 · Sarah LimProgram Management

Beyond the lessons, there's one new risk: the optical health sensor. It's a new component from a new supplier. Hoon, what do we know?

36 · Hoon JeongKernel & BSP

Very little so far, which is itself a concern. The supplier has provided a datasheet and a sample driver written for an older kernel. We don't know how mature their firmware is, or how often they plan to update it. After what happened with the fingerprint sensor on Orion Fold, I want a clear agreement on how firmware changes are communicated.

37 · Jason KwonSQE

And the sensor's calibration process on the production line. On Orion Fold, a silent format change in calibration data almost shipped broken units. I'd want the driver to reject unknown calibration formats loudly from day one, the same fix Ara made.

38 · Sarah LimProgram Management

I'll add a supplier kickoff for the sensor in September, with manufacturing engineering included, so the calibration and firmware processes are agreed before the first sample units arrive.

Onboarding a New Kernel Lead

39 · Sarah LimProgram Management

Hoon, since you're new to the watch team, what do you need to get up to speed quickly? I'd rather invest in that now than discover gaps in November.

40 · Hoon JeongKernel & BSP

Three things. First, a few hours with Dongwook to understand the Pulse Watch 3 kernel and the power management tuning, because a lot of it will carry over to the W5. Second, access to the Pulse Watch 3 crash data from the field, so I know which kernel issues users actually hit. Third, the boot traces from before and after his optimization work.

41 · Mina HwangPlatform Framework

The boot traces are already on the wiki, along with the tech share notes. I'd also recommend reading the boot loop postmortem summary. It explains the modem dependency much better than the code does.

42 · Jason KwonSQE

And I'd suggest Hoon joins the next two Pulse Watch 3 status reviews, even though he won't own anything there. Seeing how the field issues are discussed is the fastest way to learn what matters on a watch.

43 · Hoon JeongKernel & BSP

That would help a lot. Watches have very different constraints from phones: a tiny battery, very aggressive sleep states, and almost no thermal headroom. I'd rather learn those lessons from someone else's data than from my own mistakes.

44 · Sarah LimProgram Management

I'll set up the session with Dongwook this week and add you to the Pulse Watch 3 review invitations. And please don't hesitate to ask basic questions in this group. Mark said the same thing at the Atlas Lite kickoff, and those questions turned out to be some of the most useful.

Quality Gates by Milestone

45 · Jason KwonSQE

Before we finalize the schedule, I'd like to agree on what each milestone actually means. On Pulse Watch 3, 'feature complete' meant that the code was merged, but several features didn't work reliably until weeks later. That compressed our stabilization time.

46 · Sarah LimProgram Management

What would you propose?

47 · Jason KwonSQE

For feature complete: every feature is merged, passes its own functional tests at ninety-five percent or more, and has no open critical issues. For code freeze: the crash rate in a seventy-two-hour stress run is below our launch target, and the battery measurement is within ten percent of the claim. If a milestone isn't met, we say so openly rather than redefining it.

48 · Alex YooPlatform Framework

I like that, especially the last part. On Pulse Watch 3, we quietly moved a few features past feature complete, and nobody outside the team knew until it was too late to adjust the schedule.

49 · Mina HwangPlatform Framework

Could we also add weak-signal and unusual boot results to the code freeze criteria? If those tests are in the nightly suite from the start, they should be passing well before code freeze.

50 · Jason KwonSQE

Yes, that fits naturally. I'll write the full criteria for each milestone and share them by the end of September, so everyone can see them long before they matter.

Draft Milestones

51 · Sarah LimProgram Management

Here's the draft schedule. Evaluation boards in mid-September. First BSP drop October 9th, with development officially starting on October 12th. First boot on proto hardware November 16th. Feature complete March 26th. Code freeze May 14th. Factory build July 9th. Launch August 13th. Please challenge it.

52 · Jason KwonSQE

The gap between code freeze and the factory build is eight weeks, which is good. But I'd like the battery claim measurement in the plan explicitly, in February, and a second measurement after code freeze, since software changes between them always affect power.

53 · Hoon JeongKernel & BSP

Five weeks from the first BSP drop to first boot on proto hardware is reasonable, if the evaluation board work goes well. If Qorvix's drop slips, everything moves, so I'd like the same escalation trigger Hyejin uses on Atlas: five working days late means a formal replan.

54 · Mina HwangPlatform Framework

Feature complete in late March works for framework, as long as the new platform version from Google is available by January. If it slips, we'll need to discuss whether to launch on the current version instead.

55 · Sarah LimProgram Management

Noted. I'll add the platform date as a dependency with a decision point at the end of January.

Action Items and Closing

56 · Sarah LimProgram Management

Let me summarize the actions. Hoon: request two W5 evaluation boards, and document modem ordering dependencies once bring-up starts. Alex: weak-signal testing plan with Haeun's routers. Mina: setup flow design with the always-on choice and update over the phone's connection. Nari: unusual boot scenarios in the nightly suite. Jason: battery test profile with marketing. And I'll get marketing's agreement this week, invite Eunji, and schedule the sensor supplier kickoff.

57 · Jason KwonSQE

This is the first kickoff I've been to where the lessons from the last program were turned into decisions in the first hour. Thank you, Sarah.

58 · Sarah LimProgram Management

Thank you all. Our next meeting is Monday, September 14th, at 2 p.m. Korea time, once the evaluation boards are in hand. I'll send out today's decisions as a short document tonight, with the five lessons at the top, so anyone who joins the program later can see why we're doing things this way.

59 · Nari ChoiSQE

I'll start writing the unusual boot test cases this week, so they're ready to run the day the first build boots on the evaluation board.

60 · Alex YooPlatform Framework

And I'll talk to Haeun on Monday about the routers. If we can get them by October, they'll be in place before first boot on proto hardware.

교훈 1: 배터리 수명 문구

12 · Sarah LimProgram Management

교훈을 하나씩 살펴보면서 각각을 구체적인 결정으로 바꿔 봅시다. 교훈 1, 배터리 문구입니다. Pulse Watch 3에서 마케팅은 상시 표시 화면을 끈 상태로 측정한 40시간을 주장했는데, 대부분 사용자는 그걸 켜 두고 약 30시간을 썼습니다. Jason, 어떻게 이걸 피할까요?

13 · Jason KwonSQE

어떤 수치든 공개되기 전에 SQE가 마케팅과 함께 배터리 테스트 프로필을 정의해야 하고, 그 프로필은 단말에 기본으로 적용되는 설정을 써야 합니다. 상시 표시 화면이 기본으로 켜져 있다면, 주장도 그걸 켠 상태로 측정해야 합니다. 그리고 프로필을 내부에 공개해서 그 수치가 정확히 무엇을 뜻하는지 모두가 알게 하겠습니다.

14 · Mina HwangPlatform Framework

그리고 기본값을 일찍 정해야 합니다. 9월 업데이트에 추가한, 사용자가 상시 표시와 일반 화면 중 고르는 설정 화면이 효과가 좋았습니다. Pulse Watch 4에서는 그 선택을 처음부터 설정 과정에 넣겠습니다.

15 · Sarah LimProgram Management

그럼 결정은 이렇습니다. 배터리 문구는 기본 설정을 쓰고, 테스트 프로필은 연말까지 마케팅과 합의하고, 상시 표시 선택은 설정 과정의 일부로 합니다. 누군가 발표 자료에 숫자를 적기 전에 이번 주에 마케팅의 동의를 받겠습니다.

16 · Jason KwonSQE

하나 더 있습니다. 전력 효율이 40% 좋아진 칩이면 마케팅이 아주 큰 숫자를 약속하고 싶어질 겁니다. 실제 소프트웨어로 실제 하드웨어에서 측정하기 전까지는 문구를 정하지 말아 달라고 요청하고 싶은데, 그건 2월 전에는 안 될 겁니다.

교훈 2: 실제 환경의 연결성

17 · Sarah LimProgram Management

교훈 2, 저희 테스트 랩이 블루투스에 너무 우호적이었다는 점입니다. Alex?

18 · Alex YooPlatform Framework

Pulse Watch 3에서는 모든 테스트 워치가 완벽한 신호로 폰 바로 옆에 있어서, 배터리를 소모시키고 알림을 지연시킨 재연결 폭주를 한 번도 보지 못했습니다. Pulse Watch 4에서는 9월에 출시한 재연결 정책이 기준이 되고, 모든 야간 빌드가 약하고 요동치는 신호로 차폐 박스 테스트를 돌립니다.

19 · Nari ChoiSQE

블루투스 너머로 확장하고 싶습니다. Orion에서 문제가 됐던 구형 공유기와의 Wi-Fi, 그리고 수신 상태가 나쁜 지역의 LTE도요. 웨어러블은 주머니나 소매 아래에 있는 시간이 길어서, 약한 신호가 예외가 아니라 정상입니다.

20 · Alex YooPlatform Framework

동의합니다. 하은 님 팀이 상호 운용성 랩에 추가하는 구형 공유기 일부를 빌릴 수 있는지 물어보겠습니다. 그쪽 것이 이미 실제 고객의 집을 반영하고 있다면 저희만의 컬렉션을 만들 이유가 없죠.

21 · Sarah LimProgram Management

좋습니다. 결정 2입니다. 첫 부팅 빌드부터 야간 스위트에 블루투스, Wi-Fi, LTE 약신호 테스트를 넣습니다.

교훈 3: 특이한 부팅

22 · Sarah LimProgram Management

교훈 3, 부트 루프입니다. Mina, 나리 님?

23 · Mina HwangPlatform Framework

근본 원인은 느린 모뎀을 처리하지 못하는 프로비저닝 서비스였고, 그게 부팅 시퀀스의 대기 때문에 몇 년 동안 가려져 있었습니다. 동욱 님 기술 공유에서 나온 엔지니어링 교훈은 순서 제약이 왜 존재하는지 이해하지 않고는 절대 없애지 않는다는 겁니다. Pulse Watch 4에서는 모뎀에 의존하는 모든 서비스가 모뎀이 준비되지 않은 상황을 명시적으로, 테스트와 함께 처리했으면 합니다.

24 · Nari ChoiSQE
