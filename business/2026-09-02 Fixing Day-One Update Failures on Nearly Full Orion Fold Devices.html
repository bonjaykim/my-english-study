Fixing Day-One Update Failures on Nearly Full Orion Fold Devices

저장 공간이 거의 찬 Orion Fold의 출시 당일 업데이트 실패 해결

Orion Fold 출시 당일 업데이트를 통신사에 제출하기 하루 전, 저장 공간이 거의 찬 단말에서 업데이트 설치가 재부팅 후 실패하고 되돌아가는 문제가 발견되었습니다. 프로그램 관리, SQE, OTA 플랫폼, 통신사 인증 팀이 원인 분석, 압축 스냅숏과 사전 공간 확인 등 수정 방안, 통신사 제출 일정 조정을 논의합니다.

The Symptom

06 · Sarah LimProgram Management

Hyemi, what exactly happens?

07 · Hyemi SongSQE

The update downloads and installs normally, then the device reboots to apply it. On most devices, it finishes and boots into the new version. But on some devices, after the reboot, the device reports that the update couldn't be completed, rolls back to the factory software, and boots normally. Then, a few hours later, it downloads the update and tries again, with the same result.

08 · Sarah LimProgram Management

How many devices?

09 · Hyemi SongSQE

Seven out of three hundred in our validation pool, so about two point three percent. All seven had more than eighty-five percent of their storage in use. On devices with more free space, we haven't seen a single failure.

10 · Ryan ChaCarrier Certification

Would a brand-new device on launch day have that much storage in use? Most people take the update during setup, before they've installed anything.

11 · Hyemi SongSQE

Not usually, but there's one important group: people who transfer data from an old phone during setup. The transfer can easily copy a hundred gigabytes of photos and apps before the update starts. Our validation pool deliberately includes devices restored from large backups for exactly that reason.

12 · Sarah LimProgram Management

And people upgrading from a previous foldable are exactly our target customers. So this isn't an edge case for us.

Root Cause Analysis

13 · Sarah LimProgram Management

Seyoung, what do the logs show?

14 · Seyoung HanOTA Platform

Let me explain how the update works first. Orion Fold uses a virtual A/B update scheme. Instead of keeping two full copies of the system, the device keeps one, and during an update, it records the changes as a snapshot in free space on the user data partition. After the reboot, the device merges the snapshot into the system. If there isn't enough free space for the snapshot, the merge can't complete, and the device rolls back to stay safe.

15 · Jinwoo BaekOTA Platform

The update engine is supposed to check the required space before it starts, and refuse to begin if there isn't enough. That check exists. The problem is that it underestimates the space needed.

16 · Seyoung HanOTA Platform

Exactly. The estimate is calculated from the update package metadata, and it assumes the snapshot is about the same size as the changed data. But the day-one update is unusually large for a delta update: one point nine gigabytes, because it includes the fingerprint fix, the audio drop, the always-on clock fix, and several framework changes. For changes spread across many files, the snapshot ends up about forty percent larger than the estimate.

17 · Hyemi SongSQE

So the check says there's enough room, the update starts, and halfway through the merge after the reboot, it runs out of space.

18 · Seyoung HanOTA Platform

Yes. And the rollback works correctly, so no user data is lost, and the device is fine. But the user sees a failed update, and the device retries every few hours, which uses battery and data each time.

19 · Sarah LimProgram Management

At least the safety mechanism did its job. That's a relief, but it's still not a launch experience I want.

Fix Options

20 · Sarah LimProgram Management

What are the options, and which can make tomorrow's submission?

21 · Jinwoo BaekOTA Platform

There are three, and they're not mutually exclusive. One: enable compressed snapshots. The update engine in the factory build already supports compressing the snapshot, which reduces its size by roughly sixty percent. It's a setting in the update package, not a change to the device software. Two: fix the space estimate in the update engine so it accounts for scattered changes. Three: make the update package smaller by splitting it.

22 · Hyemi SongSQE

If compressed snapshots are already supported, why weren't they enabled in the first place?

23 · Seyoung HanOTA Platform

Compression makes the merge after the reboot slower, because the device has to decompress while it merges. On Orion, we measured the extra time at about forty seconds, and the product team preferred a faster update. For a normal monthly update, that trade-off is reasonable. For an unusually large update on nearly full devices, it isn't.

24 · Sarah LimProgram Management

Forty seconds once, during a day-one update, is completely acceptable. Can we enable compression for this update only?

25 · Jinwoo BaekOTA Platform

Yes. It's a flag when we generate the package. I'd regenerate the package with compression today, and SQE can rerun the full update validation on the same three hundred devices, including the seven that failed.

26 · Seyoung HanOTA Platform

With compression, the snapshot for this update would be about eight hundred megabytes instead of two point six gigabytes. Even with the estimate's error, all seven failing devices would have had enough space.

The Estimate Fix

27 · Hyemi SongSQE

Compression helps this update, but the estimate is still wrong. What happens with the next large update?

28 · Jinwoo BaekOTA Platform

That's why I want the estimate fixed properly, but in the update engine itself, which means it ships as part of an update. The earliest it can reach devices is in this day-one update, but changing the update engine in the same update that it's installing adds risk. I'd rather fix it in the first monthly update in October.

29 · Seyoung HanOTA Platform

There's also a safety improvement we can make in the update client, which is a separate app that can be updated through the app store. It could check free space with a larger margin before even downloading, and if space is tight, ask the user to free some up, instead of starting an update that's likely to fail.

30 · Sarah LimProgram Management

That's a much better experience than a silent retry loop. Can the client change be ready before launch?

31 · Seyoung HanOTA Platform

Yes. Because it's delivered through the app store, it doesn't need carrier approval. I can have it ready and tested by September 10th, more than a week before launch.

32 · Hyemi SongSQE

Please also stop the automatic retries after two failures. Retrying every few hours forever is worse than failing once with a clear message.

33 · Seyoung HanOTA Platform

Agreed. After two failures, the client will stop, show a notification explaining that more free space is needed, and offer a shortcut to the storage settings.

Submitting on Friday the fourth, ten working days ends on September 18th, which is launch day itself. That's too close.

They may be willing to start their review early. Second, if they can't finish by the seventeenth, devices on those networks would install the day-one update a few days after launch. With the client fix, nobody would be stuck in a retry loop.

Why Validation Caught It Late

42 · Jinwoo BaekOTA Platform

I want to acknowledge one thing. We should have caught this earlier. The package grew from one point one to one point nine gigabytes over the last two weeks as fixes were added, and nobody re-evaluated whether the default settings were still appropriate.

43 · Hyemi SongSQE

And on the SQE side, our update tests ran on devices with plenty of free space until the final validation. We only include the nearly full, restored devices at the very end, because setting them up takes a full day. That's backwards; the hardest cases should run first.

44 · Seyoung HanOTA Platform

We could add an automatic check to the package build: if the estimated snapshot for a near-full device exceeds a threshold, the build warns us and suggests enabling compression. That would have flagged this when the package crossed one and a half gigabytes.

45 · Sarah LimProgram Management

Good. Let's add both: the build warning, and nearly full devices in the update tests from the first candidate, not the last.

46 · Hyemi SongSQE

To make that practical, I'll keep a permanent pool of twenty restored, nearly full devices for update testing, instead of preparing them from scratch each time. That removes the one-day setup cost, which was the real reason we left them until the end.

Exposure on Other Products

47 · Sarah LimProgram Management

Is this only an Orion Fold problem, or could the same estimate error affect updates on other devices?

48 · Seyoung HanOTA Platform

The estimate logic is the same on every device that uses virtual A/B updates, which includes Orion, Orion Lite, and Nova Tab. It only becomes a problem when the update is large and the device is nearly full. Most monthly updates are between three and six hundred megabytes, so the error is small in absolute terms, and we haven't seen failures in the field.

49 · Hyemi SongSQE

But the Orion and Orion Lite OS upgrades in the winter will be much larger than a monthly update, probably three or four gigabytes. That's exactly the scenario where this would hurt most, on devices that have been in use for over a year and are often nearly full.

50 · Jinwoo BaekOTA Platform

That's a very good point, and it raises the priority of the estimate fix. If it ships in October, it will be on every Orion and Orion Lite device well before the OS upgrade starts. I'll also make sure the OS upgrade packages are generated with compression by default, regardless of the estimate.

51 · Sarah LimProgram Management

Please share that with Joanne. She's running the OS upgrade program, and Minji already flagged data migration on nearly full devices as a top risk. This fits right into that conversation.

52 · Seyoung HanOTA Platform

The update client fix will help there too. If a device doesn't have enough space for a large OS upgrade, it's much better to tell the user before downloading four gigabytes than after.

Launch Day Communication

53 · Sarah LimProgram Management

Let's also think about launch day. Even with all the fixes, some users with very full devices will see the client ask them to free up space before updating. What will they see, and what should support say?

54 · Seyoung HanOTA Platform

The notification will say how much space is needed, in simple terms, like 'about three gigabytes', and offer a button that opens the storage cleanup screen. That screen already suggests large files, unused apps, and cached data that can be removed safely.

55 · Hyemi SongSQE

I'd make sure the message doesn't sound like an error. Users who just transferred a hundred gigabytes from their old phone shouldn't feel they did something wrong. Something like 'Your new phone needs a little more space to finish setting up' is friendlier.

56 · Sarah LimProgram Management

I like that wording. I'll share the text with Customer Experience today, so support can prepare a short answer before launch.

57 · Ryan ChaCarrier Certification

I'll also include the wording in the carrier submission notes. Carrier store staff often help customers with data transfer during setup, so they're likely to be the first ones to see the message.

We submit to carriers on Friday, September 4th, one day later than planned. Ryan sends the two slow US carriers a preview tomorrow. Seyoung delivers the update client fix with the larger margin and the retry limit by September 10th. Jinwoo fixes the estimate in the update engine for October. Any objections?

원인 분석

13 · Sarah LimProgram Management

세영 님, 로그에는 뭐가 나오나요?

14 · Seyoung HanOTA Platform

먼저 업데이트가 어떻게 동작하는지 설명하겠습니다. Orion Fold는 가상 A/B 업데이트 방식을 씁니다. 시스템 전체 사본을 두 벌 유지하는 대신 한 벌만 두고, 업데이트 중에 변경 내용을 사용자 데이터 파티션의 여유 공간에 스냅숏으로 기록합니다. 재부팅 후 단말이 스냅숏을 시스템에 병합합니다. 스냅숏을 위한 여유 공간이 충분하지 않으면 병합을 완료할 수 없어서, 안전을 위해 롤백합니다.

15 · Jinwoo BaekOTA Platform

업데이트 엔진은 시작하기 전에 필요한 공간을 확인하고, 충분하지 않으면 시작을 거부해야 합니다. 그 확인은 존재합니다. 문제는 필요한 공간을 과소평가한다는 겁니다.

정확합니다. 추정치는 업데이트 패키지 메타데이터로 계산되고, 스냅숏이 변경된 데이터와 크기가 비슷하다고 가정합니다. 그런데 출시 당일 업데이트는 변경분 업데이트치고 유난히 큽니다. 지문 수정, 오디오 드롭, 상시 표시 시계 수정, 여러 프레임워크 변경이 들어가서 1.9GB입니다. 여러 파일에 퍼진 변경의 경우 스냅숏이 추정치보다 약 40% 커집니다.

공간 추정 수정

27 · Hyemi SongSQE
