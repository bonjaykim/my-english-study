03 / Business expressions

at short notice

갑작스러운 요청에도 · 짧은 준비 시간에 참여하거나 대응할 때

Thanks for making time at short notice.

응용 연습
We arranged a client call at short notice.

flag anything

필요한 사항을 짚어 알리다 · 결정이나 주의가 필요한 항목을 요청할 때

We have twenty minutes, so please flag anything that requires a decision rather than simply giving us a general status update.

응용 연습
Please flag anything that could delay approval.

narrow the scope

범위를 줄이다 · 일정과 품질을 맞추기 위해 제공 범위를 조정할 때

I would also like us to agree on the wording we use with customers if we decide to narrow the scope of the first release.

응용 연습
We may need to narrow the scope to meet the deadline.

at the expense of

무언가를 희생하면서 · 다른 중요한 가치를 훼손하는 선택을 경계할 때

However, I do not want to protect a date at the expense of trust.

응용 연습
We should not reduce costs at the expense of quality.

based on

~에 근거하여 · 판단에 사용한 자료와 근거를 밝힐 때

It only affects the regional access feature, based on the tests we have completed so far.

응용 연습
Based on customer feedback, we will simplify the form.

align on

~에 대해 합의하다 · 회의에서 의사결정의 대상과 방향을 일치시킬 때

Let us align on the decision we are actually making.

응용 연습
We need to align on the budget before contacting suppliers.

provided we

우리가 ~한다는 조건으로 · 동의하면서 필수 조건을 명시할 때

Yes, provided we explain the limits of the evidence.

응용 연습
We can proceed, provided we receive written approval.

in parallel

병행하여 · 여러 작업을 동시에 추진할 때

We can run the two workstreams in parallel, with one important dependency.

응용 연습
Design and testing can move forward in parallel.

raise a concern

우려를 제기하다 · 상대의 제안을 존중하면서 위험을 논의할 때

Can I raise a concern about the customer experience?

응용 연습
I would like to raise a concern about staffing.

outside its current scope

현재 범위 밖에 · 요청된 기능이 이번 제공 범위에 포함되지 않음을 설명할 때

We can say plainly that this pilot focuses on the headquarters reporting workflow and that regional access is outside its current scope.

응용 연습
Automated billing is outside its current scope.

go or no-go

진행 또는 중단 · 출시나 프로젝트의 최종 진행 여부를 판단할 때

Our final go or no-go review will be Wednesday, October fourteenth, at four p.m. Korea time.

응용 연습
We will make the go or no-go decision after testing.

subject to

~을 조건으로 · 제안이 특정 조건 충족을 전제로 함을 밝힐 때

We will prepare a headquarters-only pilot for October fifteenth, subject to the release criteria being met.

응용 연습
The agreement is subject to legal review.

keep us in the loop

우리에게 계속 상황을 공유하다 · 변경과 진행 상황에 관한 지속적인 공유를 요청할 때

Please keep us in the loop if the customer list changes, because we prepare an account-specific checklist for each participant.

응용 연습
Please keep us in the loop as negotiations progress.

revisit the launch date

출시일을 다시 검토하다 · 새로운 근거에 따라 기존 계획을 재검토할 때

In either case, we may need to revisit the launch date rather than adding another workaround.

응용 연습
If testing takes longer, we should revisit the launch date.

sign-off

최종 승인 · 관련 책임자가 준비 완료를 공식 확인할 때

I will communicate the final decision, and we will require both technical and operational sign-off.

응용 연습
We need Finance sign-off before placing the order.

circulate a written recap

서면 요약을 공유하다 · 회의 결과와 책임을 명확히 남길 때

I will circulate a written recap today so that the decisions are not buried in meeting notes.

응용 연습
I will circulate a written recap after the negotiation.
